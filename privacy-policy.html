Access tokens are stored securely and are only used for the purpose of publishing content. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties.

BrainBolt uses TikTok's Content Posting API to publish videos on behalf of authorized users. Our use of TikTok data is governed by TikTok's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain your access tokens only as long as necessary to provide our services. You may revoke access at any time through your TikTok account settings.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
