Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use SPTMEDYA, we may collect the following information:

  • TikTok account information (username, display name) provided through OAuth authorization
  • Content metadata (video titles, descriptions, tags) for content we publish on your behalf

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Publish video content to your authorized TikTok account
  • Monitor the status of published content
  • Improve our content creation and distribution services

3. TikTok Data

We access your TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API. We only request the minimum permissions necessary to publish content (video.publish scope). We do not access your private messages, follower lists, or personal data beyond what is required for content posting.

4. Data Storage

We store authorization tokens securely and only retain them for as long as necessary to provide our services. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your TikTok credentials and access tokens are stored securely and are never shared.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption of sensitive credentials and secure storage practices.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Revoke TikTok authorization at any time through your TikTok account settings
  • Request access to your stored data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
